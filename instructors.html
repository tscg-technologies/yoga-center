Our Pedagogical Ethics

Personalized Guidance in Every Session

At YOGA CENTER, teachers offer clear verbal instructions, visual demonstrations, and optional gentle hands-on alignment corrections only with prior student consent.

Modifications for Every Anatomy:

Poses are adapted to fit the student's body—never forcing the body to conform to an idealized shape.

Consistent Breath Rhythm:

If breathing becomes strained or gasping, instructors gently encourage resting in child’s pose.

Personal Yoga Training:

Dedicated 1-on-1 private sessions tailored to physical recovery, flexibility goals, and bespoke schedules (₹800 demo price).

Studio Harmony

Yoga Practice Guidelines

Simple etiquettes to ensure a serene, respectful, and safe sanctuary for all participants.

🕐 Arrive 10 Minutes Early

Give yourself time to settle, set up your mat, and disconnect from outer hustle before session begins.

🔇 Silent Technology

Keep mobile phones muted or placed safely in studio lockers to preserve deep acoustic calm.

👂 Listen to Your Body

Honor your limits. Avoid sharp pain or straining; take rest in Balasana (Child's Pose) whenever needed.
