Foundations of Breath

Pranayama & Breathing Exercises

Pranayama is the science of breath regulation. By consciously governing the rhythm, retention, and depth of our breathing cycles, we positively influence energy distribution and soothe bodily tension.

  • Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril): Balances mental energy and brings symmetric calm to both cerebral hemispheres.
  • Bhramari (Humming Bee Breath): Acoustic resonance that calms mental chatter and tension headaches gently.
  • Ujjayi (Victorious Ocean Breath): Builds steady rhythmic focus and internal grounding for meditation.
